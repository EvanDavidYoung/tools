Speech models

Prices come straight from OpenRouter's catalogue and are per million characters of input text. Voice strings are passed through untouched, so what's valid is whatever the upstream engine accepts — the dropdown shows what OpenRouter advertises, and the box beside it takes anything else. Provider options is raw JSON merged into provider.options.<engine>: MiniMax is seeded with {"language_boost":"Chinese"} because without it the engine auto-detects and often reads Mandarin with Cantonese phonology — swap in {"language_boost":"Chinese,Yue"} when you actually want Cantonese.
